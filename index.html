How this works

Everything you enter is saved in the office Google Sheet (tabs Tasks, Clients, Staff, Settings). Everyone with the web-app link sees the same data; the page refreshes every minute, or press ↻ Refresh. To protect client data, the owner can set an Office PIN in the Settings tab of the Google Sheet.

Pick your own name in I am at the top — this device will then show My tasks for you.

The Compliance generator fills in standard statutory due dates. Due dates are frequently extended by CBDT / CBIC / MCA notifications, so always check the official portals and correct the date in the generator before creating tasks:

  • GST portal — gst.gov.in · CBIC GST notifications
  • Income-tax e-filing — incometax.gov.in · incometaxindia.gov.in (tax calendar, notifications)
  • TRACES — tdscpc.gov.in
  • MCA — mca.gov.in
  • EPFO — epfindia.gov.in · ESIC — esic.gov.in
